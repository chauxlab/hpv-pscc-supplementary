# Supplementary File S1. Full search strategies

**Databases searched:** Europe PMC; MEDLINE (via the PubMed interface).
**Date of last search:** 13 August 2026.
**Restrictions:** none by date. Records in English or Spanish were eligible.

## Europe PMC

1. `(TITLE:"penile" OR ABSTRACT:"penile") AND (TITLE:"squamous" OR ABSTRACT:"squamous cell carcinoma") AND ("human papillomavirus" OR HPV) AND ("tumor immune microenvironment" OR "tumor microenvironment" OR "immune infiltrate" OR "immune microenvironment")`
2. `(TITLE:"penile" OR ABSTRACT:"penile") AND (TITLE:"squamous" OR ABSTRACT:"squamous cell carcinoma") AND ("PD-L1" OR "tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes" OR TIL OR CD8 OR "regulatory T cells" OR Treg OR "tumor-associated macrophages" OR TAM OR TIGIT OR "immune checkpoint")`
3. `(TITLE:"penile" OR ABSTRACT:"penile") AND (TITLE:"squamous" OR ABSTRACT:"squamous cell carcinoma") AND (immunotherapy OR "checkpoint inhibitor" OR "immune checkpoint blockade" OR pembrolizumab OR nivolumab OR atezolizumab OR PERICLES OR HERCULES)`

Records retrieved: **437** (before deduplication).

## Records identified by other methods

A bibliography curated by the authors (Paperpile export, 13 August 2026) contributed **23** eligible penile-SCC–relevant records, screened under the same eligibility criteria as the database searches and without automatic inclusion. Four items in that export were methodological citations for reporting or appraisal frameworks and were not counted as candidate records.

## Deduplication

Within-source and between-source deduplication was performed by DOI/PMID, including preprint-to-published substitutions where a peer-reviewed version was verified. Total before deduplication: **767** (437 + 307 + 23). After deduplication and substitution, **449** records were removed and **318** unique records entered title/abstract screening.

## Contextual search (not part of the PRISMA evidence flow)

A separate, complementary search on epidemiology, disease burden, and clinical guidelines (2016–August 2026) was conducted to inform the Introduction and Discussion only. These records were not subject to the eligibility criteria above and are not counted in the PRISMA flow for included studies.
