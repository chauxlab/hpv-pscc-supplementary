# Supplementary Table S2. Complete list of included studies (119 unique studies)

Two pairs of reports were versions (preprint and peer-reviewed publication) of the same underlying study; the peer-reviewed version is listed below as the citable record, with the corresponding preprint noted beneath it, giving 119 unique studies.
