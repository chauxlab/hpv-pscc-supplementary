| 29 | Apolo et al. 2024 | Phase I trial (basket; penile subgroup only) | 11 | NR | PD-1, CTLA-4, peripheral immune subsets | Penile subgroup (n = 9 evaluable, cabozantinib+nivolumab+ipilimumab): ORR 44%, median PFS 4.8 mo, median OS 6.7 mo; correlates pooled across histologies. *(non-HPV-stratified)* | low |
| 30 | Borcoman et al. 2025 | Phase 2 basket trial (penile cohort) | 11 | p16 IHC + HPV PCR typing | PD-L1 | Penile cohort did not meet its primary endpoint: ORR 18%, median OS 4.4 mo (shortest of six cohorts); HPV/PD-L1 outcome analyses pooled across SCC sites. *(non-HPV-stratified)* | low |

N is the analytic sample used for the immune analyses; for basket trials reporting a rare-tumor subgroup, N is the penile subgroup. HPV method is recorded verbatim from each source; "NR" denotes that the report does not state the assay. MMAT gives the overall quality rating (Section 2.5; Table 4). CD8, cytotoxic T lymphocyte; CPS, combined positive score; CSS, cancer-specific survival; DSS, disease-specific survival; IF, immunofluorescence; IHC, immunohistochemistry; LNM, lymph node metastasis; NR, not reported; OS, overall survival; PFS, progression-free survival; RFS, recurrence-free survival; TLS, tertiary lymphoid structure; TMA, tissue microarray; TMB, tumor mutational burden.

**Table 2.** Comparative TIME features: HPV-driven versus HPV-independent pSCC.

| Immune dimension | HPV-driven pSCC | HPV-independent pSCC | Key refs | Narrative certainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PD-L1 expression | Less uniformly PD-L1-high | Higher tumor PD-L1 expression | 10,19,20 | Moderate |
| Effector/cytotoxic (Granzyme B, PD-1) | Higher stromal Granzyme B and intratumoral PD-1 | Not specifically enriched | 11 | Low–moderate |
| Myeloid infiltrate (CD14/CD68/CD163) | Higher intratumoral myeloid densities | Not specifically enriched | 13 | Low–moderate |
| Tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) | TLS-linked antitumor architecture more often present | Less characterized | 15,26 | Low–moderate |
| Unsupervised immune classes (global phenotype) | Not reducible to HPV status alone — overlaps with HPV-independent classes | Not reducible to HPV status alone | 11 | Low |
| HLA class I downregulation / survival | Less consistently linked to survival | Partial HLA-A loss independently adverse for survival | 17 | Moderate (for this specific contrast) |
| ICI response modification by HPV | May modify PD-1 inhibitor outcomes (hypothesis-generating) | Not specifically reported | 27 | Low |

Certainty labels reproduce the narrative certainty statements already given in the Results/Discussion; no new pooled estimate was computed for this table. Reference 16 (Tang et al. 2024) is not cited in this table because it did not stratify by HPV status (Table 1) and therefore cannot support an HPV-driven-versus-independent contrast; it remains cited for TLS-prognosis findings generally (Table 3).
