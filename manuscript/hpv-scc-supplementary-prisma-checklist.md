# Supplementary File S3. Completed PRISMA 2020 checklist

This review is an integrative review (Whittemore and Knafl) with a narrative synthesis; no
meta-analysis was performed. Reporting follows PRISMA 2020 (Page et al., *BMJ* 2021;n71) adapted to
a narrative synthesis: items that presuppose statistical pooling (13f, 20b–20d, 21, 22) are marked
*Not applicable — narrative synthesis*, with the corresponding narrative element reported instead.
Section numbers refer to the manuscript.
