# Review protocol — HPV_PSCC

**Title:** The Immune Microenvironment in Penile Squamous Cell Carcinoma: Distinctions Between HPV-Driven and HPV-Independent Pathways

**Review type:** Integrative review (Whittemore and Knafl framework)
**Manuscript language:** English
**Framework:** PICO
**Search date:** 2026-08-13
**Prospective registration:** Not registered. PROSPERO does not accept integrative reviews; no retrospective registration (e.g., OSF) was pursued for this cycle.

## Review question

In patients with penile squamous cell carcinoma (pSCC), how does tumor immune microenvironment (TIME) composition — including CD8+ T-cell infiltrate, PD-L1 expression, regulatory T cells (Tregs), tumor-associated macrophages (TAMs), and other immune checkpoints — differ between HPV-driven and HPV-independent tumors, and what are the implications of this distinction for prognosis and immunotherapy response?

## PICO

- **Population:** Patients with penile squamous cell carcinoma (pSCC).
- **Intervention/Concept:** Tumor immune microenvironment (TIME) composition, stratified by HPV status (driven vs. independent).
- **Comparator/Context:** HPV-positive vs. HPV-negative/independent.
- **Outcomes:** Immune composition (CD8/TIL, PD-L1, Tregs, TAM, checkpoints), immunotherapy response, prognosis.

## Search strategy

Structured searches were executed on 2026-08-13 in two database channels (Europe PMC and MEDLINE), combining penile carcinoma terms with HPV vocabulary, TIME/immune-infiltrate descriptors, checkpoint markers, and immunotherapy/trial identifiers. Full verbatim Boolean strings for both channels are given in `searches/hpv-scc-supplementary-search-strategies.md`.

A third channel ("identified by other methods") consisted of a bibliography of 27 pSCC-relevant records curated by the authors from prior work on this topic, screened under the same eligibility rules described below (no automatic inclusion); 23 of the 27 were retained as eligible candidates after excluding 4 methodological citations (reporting/appraisal-framework references not treated as evidence candidates).

### Deduplication and substitution rules applied before the final count

1. **Exclusion of the review's own preprint** (a preprint version of this integrative review itself) from the screening corpus, as it is the review, not an evidence source.
2. **Preprint-to-published substitutions**, where a peer-reviewed version of a preprint record was identified and verified in Europe PMC/MEDLINE and substituted for the preprint (2 pairs; see Supplementary Table S2 for the resolved citations).
3. One additional preprint record (a companion paper by the same research group as reference 12) had no peer-reviewed version identified as of 2026-08-13 despite an explicit search; it was retained in the corpus as a preprint, flagged for editorial verification of publication status.

## Eligibility criteria

### Inclusion

- Studies of any design (observational, trial, case series/reports, or reviews used for contextualization) reporting TIME composition and/or HPV status in human patients with pSCC, with at least one of: CD8/tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL) density, PD-L1, Tregs, TAMs, other immune checkpoints, immunotherapy response, or prognostic immune associations.
- English or Spanish publications, without date restriction.
- Systematic reviews, narrative reviews, and meta-analyses were eligible as contextualization candidates, subject to the same screening.

### Exclusion

- **Non-human studies**, including six equine penile SCC model studies identified in the corpus; these were flagged as species-based exclusion candidates for explicit reviewer decision at screening rather than removed automatically.
- Studies unrelated to pSCC (e.g., prostate cancer, HPV-associated oral cancer used only as background context) — assessed case by case at screening.
- Methodological citations (reporting or risk-of-bias framework references) supporting only the Methods section were excluded from the evidence-screening corpus before import, as they are not candidate evidence sources.

## Appraisal tool

**MMAT 2018** (Mixed Methods Appraisal Tool), selected because the corpus mixes quantitative observational studies (the majority), clinical trials, case series/reports, and reviews.

## Contextual search scope (background only)

Not subject to this protocol's eligibility criteria; used only to inform the Introduction and Discussion, not the evidence synthesis:

- **Terms:** pSCC epidemiology and disease burden (incidence, regional prevalence, risk factors); current EAU (European Association of Urology) and ASCO/NCCN clinical guidelines for penile cancer management, including immunotherapy recommendations.
- **Period:** 2016–2026.
